# Aesthetic Vocabularies

A catalog of load-bearing directions. Each entry: worldview, palette temperament,
type voice, motion temperament, where it excels, and its failure mode (how it goes
wrong when applied lazily). Blend at most two. These are starting points for a
philosophy, not skins — the philosophy document still gets written.

## Swiss / International

Grid absolutism, objective tone, asymmetric balance, Helvetica lineage.
**Palette:** near-monochrome plus one saturated primary. **Type:** one grotesque
family, extreme size contrast (9pt captions against 96pt statements). **Motion:**
minimal, exact, no bounce. **Excels:** editorial sites, agencies, anything claiming
authority. **Failure mode:** timid size contrast — Swiss without a 6×+ scale jump is
just a boring sans-serif page.

## Editorial / Magazine

The page as a spread: serif display, drop caps, pull quotes, images breaking the
grid, generous measure. **Palette:** paper tones, ink, one editorial accent (oxblood,
forest). **Type:** high-contrast serif display over humanist text face. **Motion:**
page-turn calm; parallax at most. **Excels:** content sites, longform, portfolios,
about-pages. **Failure mode:** serif slapped on a startup template with none of the
layout courage.

## Brutalism (web)

Raw structure exposed: system fonts or aggressive mono, visible borders, unstyled-on-
purpose moments, harsh contrast. **Palette:** black, white, one alarm color.
**Type:** huge, unapologetic, sometimes distorted. **Motion:** instant or deliberately
crude. **Excels:** portfolios, culture/music sites, standing out instantly.
**Failure mode:** illegibility; brutalism still needs rigorous alignment underneath
or it reads as broken rather than defiant.

## Warm Terminal / Phosphor

CRT and early-computing nostalgia executed with modern polish: dark ground, glowing
mono type, scanline restraint. **Palette:** near-black with amber or green phosphor,
tiny chroma elsewhere. **Type:** monospace everywhere, bitmap-adjacent headers.
**Motion:** typewriter reveals, cursor blinks, instant state changes. **Excels:**
dev tools, dashboards, AI/agent products. **Failure mode:** green-on-black cliché
with bloom filters over everything; the modern version keeps glow for ≤2 elements.

## Museum Label / Gallery

The artifact is the hero; the design is wall text. Vast whitespace, tiny precise
captions, restrained palette, obsessive alignment. **Palette:** gallery white or
plinth grey, no accent or one hairline accent. **Type:** small caps, wide tracking,
one refined family. **Motion:** slow fades only. **Excels:** portfolios, product
photography, luxury. **Failure mode:** so restrained nothing establishes hierarchy —
the one large element per view is mandatory.

## Industrial / Blueprint

Engineering-drawing honesty: hairline rules, dimension ticks, registration marks,
annotation type. **Palette:** blueprint blue or graphite plus a safety orange.
**Type:** DIN-flavored sans plus a mono for annotations. **Motion:** draw-on lines,
plotter-style reveals. **Excels:** technical products, hardware, data-heavy marketing.
**Failure mode:** decoration cosplay — ticks and crosshairs that measure nothing.

## Organic / Hand

Human marks against digital ground: irregular blob shapes, hand-drawn underlines,
paper grain, imperfect rotation (±1–2°). **Palette:** warm naturals with one juicy
accent. **Type:** a friendly serif or rounded sans; never a script font for body.
**Motion:** springy, slightly overshooting, alive. **Excels:** consumer brands,
education, food, anything fighting corporate coldness. **Failure mode:** clip-art
quirkiness; the hand-made elements must be few and consistent in tool (all one "pen").

## Retro-Futurist / Vapor

Chrome gradients, grid horizons, airbrush glow — the future as imagined from the
past, executed knowingly. **Palette:** sunset ramps, deep violets, cyan edge-light.
**Type:** extended geometric sans, chrome or outline display. **Motion:** slow drift,
lens flares earned not sprayed. **Excels:** music, games, event sites. **Failure
mode:** this is the direction closest to AI-default gradient slop — it only works
with period-accurate discipline (reference real 1982–1992 artifacts).

## Data-Ink / Tufte

Maximal information per pixel: no chart junk, hairline axes, direct labeling, small
multiples. **Palette:** greys with semantic color used only for data. **Type:** one
quiet sans, tabular numerals mandatory. **Motion:** none except state transition of
the data itself. **Excels:** dashboards, reports, scientific viz. **Failure mode:**
mistaking austerity for hierarchy — the key number must still be unmissable.

## Toy / Playful

Fat rounded shapes, bouncy physics, saturated primaries, oversized touch targets.
**Palette:** 3–4 saturated hues on cream or deep navy, never on white. **Type:**
rounded or chunky geometric, big. **Motion:** squash-and-stretch, springs with
visible overshoot. **Excels:** games, kids' products, delight-first consumer apps.
**Failure mode:** noise — playful still needs one calm layer (usually the
background) or nothing reads.

## Luxury / Quiet

Wealth whispers: thin serifs, hairline rules, enormous margins, near-invisible
interactions. **Palette:** ivory, charcoal, one metallic-adjacent tone used almost
never. **Type:** high-contrast didone or refined garalde, wide tracking on caps.
**Motion:** 400–600ms fades, nothing faster, nothing bouncy. **Excels:** fashion,
jewelry, hospitality, premium positioning. **Failure mode:** center-aligning
everything and calling it elegant; luxury layouts are asymmetric and confident.

---

## Choosing

Match the direction to the **job and audience**, not to personal taste: authority →
Swiss or Museum; density → Data-Ink or Warm Terminal; warmth → Organic or Toy;
prestige → Luxury or Editorial; defiance → Brutalism. Then push it one notch further
than feels safe — directions fail far more often from timidity than from excess.
