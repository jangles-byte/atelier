The same 20 px of travel, measured per frame

Card entrance sampled every animation frame in Chrome, normalized to 0–1. Both pages are byte-identical except for their motion code. The dashed line is 300ms linear; the solid line is 260ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1).

The eased card is 4.4× further along at 40ms and visually finished (96.5%) by 133ms, while the linear card is still at 44% — this is what "snappy" measures as. The linear curve's equal per-frame increments (0.055 every frame, without deviation) are the signature of uniform velocity: the reason it reads as mechanical rather than physical.
